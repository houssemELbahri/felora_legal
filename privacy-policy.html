Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Felora (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you play the Game on iOS or Android. Felora is published by Houssem Bahri, an independent developer based in Tunisia, who is responsible for your data.

We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the Game. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Information we collect

a. Game account (automatic)

When you first open Felora, we create an anonymous account for you. It is identified only by a random ID. You do not need to give a name, email or phone number to play.

b. Game progress (automatic)

Your island is saved to our servers so it is not lost: your game state, island name, player level and the dates it was last saved. This is what lets you restore your island on a new phone.

c. Sign-in details (optional)

If you choose to save your island to an account, you can link:

  • Sign in with Apple: we receive an Apple user ID and the email Apple shares with us. If you choose “Hide My Email”, we only receive an Apple relay address, never your real email.
  • Email: we receive the email address you enter, to send you a one-time 6-digit code.

We use these only to let you sign in and restore your island. We never send marketing emails.

d. Crash and diagnostic data (automatic)

When the Game crashes or hits an error, we receive a technical report so we can fix it. It can include: the error and the part of the code where it happened, the screens visited just before, the device model, the operating system and app version, and your anonymous account ID. We have configured our crash reporting so that it does not collect your IP address or your device name.

e. Purchases

If the Game offers in-app purchases (for example, gems), payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment card details. We only receive confirmation of what was bought, to deliver it in the Game.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the Game and save your progress
  • To let you restore your island on another device
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs
  • To check purchases and prevent cheating or abuse

The legal basis for this processing is the performance of our agreement with you (providing the Game) and our legitimate interest in keeping the Game working and secure.

4. Data storage and security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each player can only access their own save, and access is enforced on the server. Our providers may store data in countries other than yours; when they do, they use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and game progress: as long as your account exists. Deleting your account erases them immediately.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. You can delete your account and all your game data at any time, directly in the Game: Profile → Delete account and data. See Delete your account for other options.

For any other request, email us at elbahri.dev@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

Felora is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and tell you in the Game if the changes are important.
