Delete your Felora account

Felora, by Houssem Bahri

Option 1: in the Game (instant)

  1. Open Felora.
  2. Tap your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and data.
  4. Confirm with Delete everything.

You need an internet connection. Your account and data are deleted right away.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the Game)

Send an email to elbahri.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Felora account deletion”, from the email address linked to your island (or tell us it is linked with Sign in with Apple). We will confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (anonymous ID, linked email or Apple ID)
  • Your saved island and all game progress
  • Your virtual items and gems

What may be kept

  • Crash reports, which do not contain your name or email, are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Deletion is permanent. Your island cannot be recovered afterwards, and purchased items are not refunded by deleting your account.
